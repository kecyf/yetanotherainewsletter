# gemma 4 goes open with a 1m context; open weights clear 80% on swe-bench

quiet weekend, loud monday. six kept from nine fleet proposals plus one by hand: two lab releases that change what you can run on your own hardware, one benchmark number i reran before believing, two tools, and a consultation deadline the eu will not extend.

## labs

### 1. [Gemma 4: open weights, 1M-token context, and a 9B model that fits on a laptop](https://deepmind.google/discover/blog/gemma-4-open-models-long-context/)

the 9b variant keeps recall above 90% at 512k tokens on their needle tests. if it holds up, whole-repo context stops being an api-only feature.

`1 · deepmind.google · scout-labs`

### 2. [Mistral adds a per-request reasoning budget to its API](https://docs.mistral.ai/capabilities/reasoning/budget/)

you cap thinking tokens per call instead of picking a model tier. it's the first lab to price reasoning as a dial, and the others will copy it.

`2 · docs.mistral.ai · scout-labs`

## sota

### 3. [OpenHands-R2: an open-weights agent reaches 81.4% on SWE-bench Verified](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14421)

first open model above 80%, with the harness and trajectories published. the gap to closed frontier models is now under four points.

`3 · arxiv.org · scout-arxiv`

## tools

### 4. [llama.cpp b5120: Gemma 4 support and draft-model speculative decoding on Metal](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/releases/tag/b5120)

day-one support for item 1, plus roughly 1.8× tokens per second on apple silicon when you pair the 9b with its 1b draft model.

`4 · github.com · scout-github`

### 5. [llm 0.31 runs tool calls inside a WASM sandbox by default](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Oct/25/llm-tools-sandbox/)

tool calls from untrusted prompts no longer touch your filesystem unless you opt in. a sane default that most agent clis still lack.

`5 · simonwillison.net · kevin · added by hand`

## timeline

### 6. [EU AI Office consultation on serious-incident reporting for GPAI models closes 2026-11-20](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/consultations/gpai-serious-incident-reporting)

this sets what counts as an incident and the reporting window. if you fine-tune and redistribute a general-purpose model, it applies to you.

`6 · digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu · scout-calendar`

## signal

> the per-request reasoning budget in item 2 is the quiet pricing shift of the quarter. when every lab matches it, reasoning stops being sold by model tier, and how you cap thinking per call becomes the number that matters.
>
> — kevin
